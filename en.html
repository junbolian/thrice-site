Models keep getting stronger. That does not mean a company can use them.

A company cannot use them when its data has not been put in order, its metrics have not been defined, nobody can tell whether a result is right, and nobody is accountable when it is wrong. These four things are the work Thrice does.

Four situations you may recognize

  1. You do not know what to do first

    Everyone says to use AI. It is not clear which task in your own company to start with, or what result would count as useful.

  2. You cannot find the right person, or judge a candidate

    You want to hire someone who knows AI. Nobody suitable is available locally, and when a candidate comes from elsewhere you cannot tell whether they can really do the work.

  3. You tried, and saw no result

    You hired someone or bought software, and months later there is still no result. The data is spread across the inventory system, the finance system and many spreadsheets, and different departments calculate the same figure differently.

  4. It was built, and nobody can say whether it is right

    You tried outsourcing. Quotes ranged from a few thousand RMB to several million, and after delivery nobody could say whether the answers were correct.

These four problems are connected. When the task is not defined, there is no standard for hiring and no basis for acceptance. Solving only one of them still produces no result.

Past projects

The founder led the projects below at a retail chain. The company is a pharmacy chain with about 160 directly operated stores, and the projects cover pricing, assortment and the store network.

Product discontinuation agent

Background
Every quarter the company has to decide which products to discontinue. The work used to take more than 100 hours a quarter.
Approach
The founder built a product discontinuation agent, an AI program that follows rules to complete a task of several steps without interruption. It places each product in a group of products that can substitute for one another, scores the candidates for discontinuation by sales volume, gross profit, price and trend, and applies protection rules for chronic-disease medicines, category coverage and the main products sold online. Staff accept or reject each suggestion and give a reason. The reasons are turned into corrected rules for the next round of suggestions.
Result
The agent has been running for more than six months. More than 95% of its suggestions are accepted, and the quarterly discontinuation work has fallen from more than 100 hours to under 1 hour.

This is the result of one project at one company. The result your project has to reach is set in the diagnosis and written into the acceptance tests.

Entering drug package inserts

Background
Drug package inserts have to be entered into the system. Paper inserts are blurred, their layouts are complex, and typing them in by hand takes a long time.
Approach
A staff member photographs the insert. The system reads the text and arranges it into fields such as generic name, ingredients, indications, dosage and approval number. Anything it cannot read clearly is flagged, and a staff member checks it before the record is saved.

Online versus in-store prices

Background
The same product has different prices in the stores and on online platforms. The price table is updated every week, and comparing it line by line by hand is not practical. Management has to judge which parts of the current price arrangement are reasonable and which are not.
Approach
Every week, store prices and the prices on two online platforms are put in one table that keeps the last four weeks. The gap is calculated for each product, and the products and categories with large gaps are identified. The analysis report, written for staff and management, states where the current arrangement is reasonable and where it is not, and recommends changes.
Delivered
An analysis report, and a dashboard that can be clicked through by category, platform and week.

Store network assessment

Background
Management has to judge which stores need a change and where to open new ones. The operating data sits in monthly reports and the locations on a map, and the two used to be looked at separately.
Approach
Twelve months of operating data for each store are joined with its coordinates. On the map, stores are colored by performance, and stores close to one another and areas without a store are marked. The assessment report is written for management and comes with a store table that can be filtered and exported.
Note
The assessment uses operating data and location. A store may also perform poorly because of staffing or a change in nearby competition, and management judges that part from what it knows on the ground.

Six principles we work by

  • One thing at a time, finished

    What we will do, what we will not do and what counts as done are written down before work starts. The project has a fixed price. We do not charge by headcount or by the day.

  • You check the result

    Acceptance depends on the pass rate on the tests, not on a demonstration and not on what we say.

  • Someone stays who can run it

    When the project ends, someone in your company must be able to use the system and change it. Our engineers guide and train the person who takes over. We select people only by the result of a practical test: completing a task within a time limit on realistic data.

  • You decide how your data is used

    The model can be called through an API or deployed on your own servers. With an API, the agreement states in advance which data may be sent to the model. We ask only for read-only access to your business systems and keep no copies. A confidentiality and data processing agreement is signed before we start on site.

  • No promises we cannot keep

    We do not promise how much your sales will grow with AI. We promise that the results written in the contract pass acceptance.

  • No software you do not need

    We do not sell general-purpose software. When software already on the market is good enough, we help you get it into use.

Common questions

Why not hire someone ourselves, or simply buy software?

Hiring on your own has two difficulties. Experienced people are not available locally, and once someone is hired it is hard to judge whether they can really do the work. That person also has to face unorganized data and an undefined task alone. With software, the difficulty is the data. Whether a feature such as data Q&A gives correct answers depends on whether the company has a single formula for each metric, and software cannot be put to use while the data is unorganized. Thrice first puts the data and the metrics in order and gets the first thing done. Then we help you put a person in place, or get the software you bought into use.

Engineers all write code with AI now. How are you different from other providers?

In coding speed we are no different. We use the same tools. The difference lies in three things outside the tools: turning what you say into metric definitions that can be calculated, knowing your company’s data, and checking the queries the AI writes. Every project is accepted with tests whose answers you have confirmed, and this is written into the contract.

AI changes fast. Will a system built now become outdated?

Models will be replaced. Your data, your metric formulas and your acceptance tests remain valid when the model changes. After a change of model or system, the same tests are run again, and the system stays in use only if it passes.

Does the model have to be deployed on our own servers?

No. You can call a model provider’s API directly, which is fast to launch and low in cost. Private deployment is for cases where the data cannot leave your internal network. With an API, the agreement states in advance which data may be sent to the model. Both options are accepted with the same set of tests.

What does the project need from us?

Three things. First, name one person in charge, who decides how each metric is calculated and which number is the reference, and signs off. Second, arrange two or three interviews of about an hour each, with you, the relevant department heads and the person who manages your systems. Third, provide read-only access to the data and a few of the reports you use now.

We are not in Wenzhou. Can you still work with us?

Yes. Projects outside Wenzhou are done in three trips, and travel costs are charged separately.

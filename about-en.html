识时务者为俊杰

The Thrice motto: “Those who understand the times are the people of ability.” It comes from the words Sima Hui used when he recommended Zhuge Liang to Liu Bei, recorded in the Xiangyang Ji and quoted in Pei Songzhi’s annotations to the Records of the Three Kingdoms. Its point is that at a turning point, what matters is choosing the right people.

About Thrice

The Chinese name, 三顾 (Sangu), means “three visits”. It comes from the story in which Liu Bei went to Zhuge Liang’s cottage three times to ask him to serve. The name describes two things we do. First, we make sure your company has people who can do the work: we teach the staff you already have, and when no one suitable is there, we test the candidates you are hiring. Second, every job we do has three steps: diagnosis, project and handover. The English name, Thrice, means “three times”.

The diagnosis report written in the first step is called the Longzhong Plan, after the plan Zhuge Liang set out for Liu Bei when they met. It states what to do first, what not to do, and what counts as done.

Founder

The founder is the Chief Data and Analytics Officer of a retail chain, where he leads the company’s AI and digital reform. The projects cover pricing, assortment and the store network for about 160 directly operated stores. He is a PhD student at the Institute of Operations Research and Analytics, National University of Singapore, and his research combines large language models with operations management. His paper on checking the output of large language models has been accepted at NeurIPS 2026, an international conference on artificial intelligence. Thrice’s methods for diagnosis and acceptance come from these projects and this research.

Engineers

Every engineer who delivers our work has passed a hands-on test: complete a task within a time limit on realistic data. Any AI tool may be used, and only the result counts.

A first book on AI for your staff

An Introduction to Artificial Intelligence: Principles, Tools and Everyday Practice is written by Thrice and used as the textbook in staff training. Readers need no programming and no mathematics. The book is in Chinese.

What the book covers

  1. Chapters 1 to 3What artificial intelligence is, the stages it has gone through in more than seventy years, how a large language model produces an answer, and why the answer is sometimes wrong.
  2. Chapters 4 and 5How to choose among the main tools, and what to do the first time you use one.
  3. Chapters 6 to 9Prompts, agents, Skills and Harnesses, each chapter harder than the one before.
  4. Chapters 10 and 11Worked examples from retail and office work, then data security and rules of use.
